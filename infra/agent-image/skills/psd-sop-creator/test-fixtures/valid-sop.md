## Title

Printer & Copier Allocation, Upkeep & Maintenance

## Scope

All schools and administrative buildings

## Procedure

To reduce costs and provide equitable access to print and copy services, all
schools will be allocated printers and copiers based on a formula. This
procedure is not intended to reduce printing but to do so in the most economical
method possible.

**Elementary & Middle Schools:** 6 printers (1 color, 5 black and white), 2 copiers

**Henderson Bay High School:** 6 printers (1 color, 5 black and white), 1 copier

**GHHS & PHS High Schools:** 14 printers (2 color, 12 black and white), 4 copiers

**Administration Offices:** No personal printers; copiers only, based on building size

Exceptions: CTE or other departments may add specialty printers to programs —
photo, large format, 3-D, or braille printers. Any exception must be
pre-approved by the Technology Department before purchase.

No printers or copiers may be added to any district facility outside this
allocation, regardless of funding options.

## Safety Considerations

All maintenance to printers and copiers will only be done by district technology
staff or vendors hired by the district. No other PSD staff member should attempt
to repair a printer or copier outside routine upkeep such as clearing a paper
jam or replacing a toner cartridge.

## Quality Control

Building Leadership Teams (BLTs) determine all school building printer locations
in collaboration with the building administrator.
