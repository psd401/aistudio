# Definitions tab

The only prose in the workbook. Factual notes only — no interpretation of this
school's results.

**Quartiles are local.** A = lowest 25%, D = highest 25%, computed separately
within each column's own population: each classroom over its own students,
School over the school's matched students in that grade, District over all
matched students. **A classroom's "A" students are therefore not the same cut as
the district's "A" students** — a classroom whose students are all strong still
has an A quartile.

**Matched students only.** A growth cell counts students with both a baseline
and an ending score for that measure, grade and year. Every value has an
adjacent n. `—` means n = 0.

**Reading Raw vs PR.** Raw is the score change; PR is the change in national
percentile rank. Positive Raw with negative PR means the students grew, but more
slowly than the national norm group — both columns are needed to see that.

**National percentiles** come from DIBELS 8th Edition national norms, University
of Oregon Technical Report 2201 (2021-22). Percentiles are clamped to 1-99.

**Windows.** State which window this report used (Fall→Spring, Fall→Winter, or
Fall status). K DIBELS baseline is Winter, where Fall is not administered;
Grade 1 ORF baseline is Winter, because ORF starts mid-year.

**Subgroups** use current-status flags (Low Income, Special Education) at the
"All" level for School and District.

**Multi-grade classrooms** (2/3, 4/5 splits) appear on both grade tabs with
small n. That is expected.

**Not for public release.** Internal use for principals and R&A.

If the norms asset was unavailable, add: *National PR columns are omitted from
this report because the DIBELS norms table was not available; raw score change
is shown instead.*
