# What We’re Learning

Every build ends with a wrap-up, and every wrap-up is published here: the builds we kept, the ones we stopped, and the ones we are running again.

Teams write their wrap-ups in about twenty minutes at the end of week 6. We publish them as the teams wrote them, with student and staff details removed.

## The current focus

Each quarter, Superintendent Krestin Bahr sets the focus in three sentences. For this cycle:

> This cycle is about exploration and innovation: learn what the AI tools we already have can do, and use them to improve one process or practice that serves your team’s goals. The tools are changing faster than any training plan can keep up with, and the quickest way to learn them is to use them on real work. Whatever you build, student data privacy and the relationships our schools are built on come first.

## How the sharing works

- **Kept builds become tools.** A kept build usually becomes a skill in the PSD AI Agent or a tool in AI Studio, and its wrap-up links to it, so you can use it as well as read about it.
- **Inside Peninsula.** Full wrap-ups are open to all staff. Every Monday, cabinet gets a five-point summary of what is moving, what is stuck, and where teams are working on the same thing. At the end of each quarter, a longer write-up covers the whole cycle.
- **Outside Peninsula.** Public versions of the wrap-ups, with student and staff details removed, are published on this page. Other districts are welcome to use anything here, including what didn’t work.

## Why we publish stopped builds

Most organizations quietly drop what doesn’t work, and what they learned goes with it. We publish stopped builds in the same detail as the ones we keep, so other teams don’t repeat them. If this page only ever shows successes, something is wrong, and we want to [hear about it](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfXMAZqz4haz2TpK6DDSMejKd4W400BaT47i5pRU7_EdlZSBQ/viewform).
