# The Open Adaptive District

A protocol for AI work. A team of three to six staff picks one problem, spends six weeks building a better way to handle it, and publishes what happened, including what didn't work. We run it at Peninsula, and any district can adopt it.

[Start here](https://psd401.ai/open-adaptive-district/start-here) · [Read the playbook →](https://psd401.ai/open-adaptive-district/playbook)

## The cycle

Six weeks of work, then two weeks between cycles. A team runs one build per cycle.

Week 1

### Plan

Pick one problem and write a one-page build plan.

Weeks 2–5

### Do

Build it and use it in real work. Post a check-in every Friday.

Week 6

### Study & share

The agent drafts the wrap-up. The team makes the call:

- Keep it
- Stop it
- Run it again

Weeks 7–8

### Between cycles

Leaders read every wrap-up and set the next focus.

## Read the protocol

5 documents

Start Here is the only required reading. The rest are there when a team needs them. Other districts are welcome to copy and adapt all five.

- **01** [The Open Adaptive District — Start Here](https://psd401.ai/open-adaptive-district/start-here): What the protocol is, why we run it, and how a cycle works. Five minutes, and the only required reading.
- **02** [The Playbook](https://psd401.ai/open-adaptive-district/playbook): The three documents a team uses (build plan, weekly check-in and wrap-up) with templates.
- **03** [What We’re Learning](https://psd401.ai/open-adaptive-district/what-were-learning): Every team's wrap-up as it is published, including the builds that were stopped.
- **04** [The Deep Dive](https://psd401.ai/open-adaptive-district/deep-dive): The reasoning and research behind the design, for anyone who wants it.
- **05** [Get Started — One Page](https://psd401.ai/open-adaptive-district/get-started): The whole cycle as twelve steps on one page, for printing.
