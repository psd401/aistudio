# Quarterly focus

*Written each quarter by the superintendent, in the weeks between cycles.*

## The template — three sentences (Start Here, psd401.ai/open-adaptive-district/start-here)

> 1. **What we are after.**
> 2. **Why it matters now.**
> 3. **What we will not compromise on.**

Teams choose their own builds within that focus. Nobody assigns them.

## The current focus — Cycle 1, fall 2026 (What We're Learning, verbatim)

> This cycle is about exploration and innovation: learn what the AI tools we already have can do, and use them to improve one process or practice that serves your team’s goals. The tools are changing faster than any training plan can keep up with, and the quickest way to learn them is to use them on real work. Whatever you build, student data privacy and the relationships our schools are built on come first.

The focus can change each quarter — check <https://psd401.ai/open-adaptive-district/what-were-learning> for the current one.

## Drafting notes

- **Three sentences.** "Anything longer turns into a task list, and teams stop choosing their own work." (The Deep Dive)
- **It says what we are after and why; teams decide how.**
- **The test:** a team can read it once and think of two or three builds.
- **It always points back to what we already value** — grade-level learning for every student, MTSS, UDL, and the commitments in Peninsula 2030.
