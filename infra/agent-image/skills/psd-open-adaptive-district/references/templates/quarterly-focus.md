# Quarterly Focus

*The only document the superintendent owes the cycle. Written in the between-cycle weeks, before the next cycle starts.*

## The template — three sentences

> 1. **What we're after** this quarter.
> 2. **Why it matters right now.**
> 3. **What we will never compromise on.**

That's it. Teams pick their own builds inside that focus — nobody hands them a to-do list.

## Drafting notes

- **Three sentences. Not four.** Anything longer becomes a to-do list, and to-do lists kill ownership.
- **It sets the *what* and the *why*; teams own the *how*.**
- **The test:** if a team can read it once and immediately picture two or three builds they could run, it's working.
- **It always points at what the district already values** — grade-level learning for every student, the supports of MTSS, the access of UDL, the promises of Peninsula 2030. AI changes the *how*. It never changes the *what*.
